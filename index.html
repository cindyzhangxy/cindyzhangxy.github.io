Xiao Yu Cindy Zhang

Biomedical AI Researcher | Clinical NLP, LLM Evaluation & Precision Medicine

Hi, I’m Cindy.

I’m a PhD candidate in Bioinformatics at the University of British Columbia (UBC), co-supervised by Prof. Wyeth W. Wasserman and Prof. Jian Zhu.

My background brings together healthcare, pharmacogenomics, biomedical AI, and machine learning. I hold an MSc in Bioinformatics, a Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD), and a BSc in Pharmacy. Before moving into research, I practiced as a registered pharmacist, providing direct patient care.

My research focuses on large language models (LLMs), biomedical natural language processing, and auditable AI systems for rare-disease diagnosis. I develop LLM-based pipelines for extracting and structuring clinical evidence from unstructured narratives, alongside embedding-based methods for phenotype representation and diagnostic retrieval. My work spans representation learning, dense retrieval, and ontology-based reasoning, with particular attention to model capabilities, failure modes, and the traceability of predictions to supporting clinical evidence.

Drawing on my clinical background and training in bioinformatics, I aim to develop AI systems that connect clinical evidence with biomedical research to improve patient care and quality of life.
